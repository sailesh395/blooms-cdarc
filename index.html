What we do

Everything your child needs, in one place

Every programme starts with an assessment, then a personalised plan reviewed with you at every step.

Occupational Therapy

Building everyday skills — fine motor, self-care, handwriting, sensory regulation and play — so children grow independent and confident.

Physiotherapy

Improving strength, balance, posture and mobility for children with motor delays, cerebral palsy and neuromuscular conditions.

Speech & Language Therapy

Helping children communicate — speech clarity, language, feeding and social communication for a stronger voice.

Special School

Individualised special education for children who learn differently — structured, patient and goal-based classrooms.

Hostel Facility

Safe, supervised residential care for outstation families — with daily therapy, meals and a caring, home-like routine.

Developmental Assessment

A thorough first evaluation to understand your child's strengths and needs — and to shape the right plan from day one.

Questions parents ask us

How do I know if my child needs therapy?

If your child is missing developmental milestones — late speech, difficulty walking or with fine motor tasks, trouble focusing, or challenges with social interaction — an assessment can help. Early support gives the best outcomes, so it's always better to check.

What happens at the first visit?

We begin with a developmental assessment to understand your child's strengths and needs. We then discuss a personalised plan with you and agree on goals together before therapy begins.

Do you offer therapy for outstation families?

Yes. Our hostel facility provides safe, supervised residential care with daily therapy for children whose families live outside Tirupati.
