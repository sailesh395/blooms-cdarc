Step one

Assessment & Early Intervention

Every journey begins with understanding. Our experts assess your child's development, screen carefully for conditions such as ASD and ADHD, and identify where support will help most. The earlier we begin, the greater the difference we can make.

  • Assessments by qualified experts
  • Identification of ASD & ADHD
  • Early intervention programmes
  • Milestone tracking & improvement
  • Parent counselling & regular follow-ups
Therapy

Occupational Therapy

Occupational therapy helps children build the everyday skills that make them independent — dressing, eating, handwriting, fine-motor control and play. Through purposeful, playful activities we help each child do the things that matter to them, at home and at school.

  • Fine & gross motor skills
  • Self-care and daily living skills
  • Handwriting & school readiness
  • Sensory integration therapy
Therapy

Speech & Language Therapy

Communication is the key to connection. Our speech therapists help children develop clear speech, understand and use language, and build the social communication skills they need to express themselves and be understood.

  • Speech clarity & articulation
  • Language understanding & use
  • Social communication skills
  • Feeding & oral-motor support
Therapy

Physiotherapy

For children with motor delays, cerebral palsy or neuromuscular conditions, physiotherapy builds strength, balance, posture and mobility — helping them move with more freedom and confidence.

  • Strength & balance training
  • Posture & mobility support
  • Gross-motor development
  • Support for cerebral palsy & motor delays
