Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Blooms Child Development & Rehabilitation Centre ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you contact us through our enquiry form, WhatsApp, phone or email, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and details you choose to share about your child, so that we can respond and provide care.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange assessments and appointments, and provide our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

WhatsApp & messaging

Our enquiry form and chat button open WhatsApp so you can message us directly. Messages you send are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

Children's information

We treat information about children with particular care and confidentiality, and share it only with the professionals involved in your child's care.

Cookies & analytics

This website may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by contacting info@bloomscdarc.com.
