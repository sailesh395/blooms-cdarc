Caring for children, walking with families

Blooms Child Development & Rehabilitation Centre is one of Tirupati's trusted centres for children with special needs — bringing therapy, special education and compassionate care together under one roof.

Our story

A centre built around your child's comfort

At Blooms, focusing on your child's health and growth is our first aim. We provide effective, personalised occupational therapy, physiotherapy and speech treatment in a space designed with the child in mind.

Our therapy rooms are ergonomically set up for comfort, with total attention paid to hygiene and sanitation. Every child is assured the best care in a calm, non-stressful atmosphere — because progress happens when a child feels safe and happy.

Our Mission

To help every child reach their fullest potential through personalised, evidence-informed therapy and education — while supporting and guiding their families every step of the way.

Our Vision

A community where children with special needs are understood, included and empowered — where early support is within every family's reach.
