A Safe, Supportive & Enabling Living Environment

Our residential campus in Tirupati offers a warm, homely, and secure environment designed specifically around the daily needs of children with Autism. With comfortable living spaces, dedicated residential rooms, dining facilities, outdoor play areas, a walking track, and spacious therapy areas, children have opportunities to relax, learn, play, and develop independence.

Comprehensive Facilities

Thoughtfully Planned for Comfort & Independence

Daily Living Skills

Pictorial cues in washrooms and dedicated spaces for grooming, self-care, and routine building encourage everyday confidence.

Recreation & Structured Play
