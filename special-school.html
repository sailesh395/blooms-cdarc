A special school where every child belongs

Individualised special education that meets children where they are — with a structured daily curriculum, therapy woven into the day, and a safe hostel for families who travel from outside Tirupati.

Special education

Learning built around your child

Children who learn differently deserve a classroom that adapts to them — not the other way around. Our special educators create individual learning plans, teach in small, patient steps, and celebrate every milestone along the way.

  • Individualised education plans
  • Daily curriculum & academic activities
  • Vocational training for older children
  • Outdoor & child-oriented activities
  • One-to-one and small-group sessions

Hostel facility

A safe home away from home

For families who live outside Tirupati, our hostel offers safe, supervised residential care — with daily therapy, nutritious meals and a warm, home-like routine. Your child is cared for around the clock, so the whole family has peace of mind.

  • Safe, supervised residential care
  • Daily therapy & structured routine
  • Nutritious meals & day care services
  • Caring staff and a home-like environment
  • Short-term respite care (days to weeks) for children and adults with autism — to support parents in emergencies
